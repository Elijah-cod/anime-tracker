# Anime Tracker Product

## Register

product

## Users

Anime fans managing an active watch list across phones and desktop browsers. They want to resume a show, log an episode, discover a title, check a release, or leave a short comment without navigating through a reporting dashboard.

## Product Purpose

Anime Tracker is a personal command center for watch progress, queues, release schedules, discovery, and anime-specific discussion. Success means the next useful action is obvious, updates feel immediate, and each account sees only its own tracking data.

## Brand Personality

Focused, calm, and cinematic. The product should feel familiar enough to trust immediately, with personality coming from anime artwork and a restrained blue-violet identity rather than decorative interface effects.

## Anti-references

- Marketing-style dashboard copy inside an authenticated product.
- Oversized metric cards, repeated nested cards, and excessive rounded containers.
- Technical implementation labels such as framework names or optimistic UI terminology.
- Poster grids that truncate titles too aggressively or squeeze controls into narrow columns.
- Decorative gradients, glass effects, and animations that compete with content.
- Social features that imply capabilities the backend does not actually support.

## Design Principles

1. Put anime artwork and the next action first.
2. Organize each route around one primary job instead of showing every feature at once.
3. Keep tracking interactions immediate, clear, and reversible.
4. Give mobile users the same core workflows through a persistent bottom navigation.
5. Show real account and anime data honestly, with useful empty and error states.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA contrast and interaction behavior. Preserve visible keyboard focus, semantic controls, touch targets of at least 44 pixels, reduced-motion preferences, text alternatives for artwork, and status cues that do not depend on color alone.
